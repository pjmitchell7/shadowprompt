Why Not LLM-as-a-Judge Guardrails?

            

              Traditional guardrails call an auxiliary LLM (like Llama Guard) to check every user prompt. In high-throughput production, adding 600–1,200ms destroys user experience and doubles inference bills. Furthermore, model tokenizers often strip or normalize invisible characters, rendering them blind to steganography. ShadowPrompt inspects the raw Unicode stream in memory in 0.06ms.
