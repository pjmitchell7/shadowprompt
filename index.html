Why Not LLM-as-a-Judge?

Traditional guardrails call an LLM to check another LLM. This adds 600–1200ms latency, doubles API cost, and fails against steganography because model tokenizers strip or alter invisible codepoints during pre-tokenization. ShadowPrompt inspects the raw Unicode stream in memory in 0.06ms.

Why the HoneyPot Sandbox?

Returning HTTP 403 gives attackers immediate feedback to iterate until they bypass your filter. ShadowPrompt's HoneyPot returns plausible synthetic outputs seeded with cryptographic canary tokens. When leaked, SecOps receives 100% conclusive proof of an exfiltration attempt.
